Each tap moves one tile. The first delivery will wake the shadow and light the house.

Four visual signals

  1. Courier: a short step and warm lantern pulse on every tap.
  2. Shadow: a slower glide and violet ripple once a house is lit.
  3. House: windows and doorstep glow, with a gold DONE marker.
  4. Roadblock: crates shake, then break into dust when repaired.
