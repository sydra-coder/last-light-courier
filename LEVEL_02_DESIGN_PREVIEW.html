The striped tiles are collapsed streets. Deliver to Lantern Row first, then decide how to cross the shadow's patrol.

Tap one neighboring tile to begin a route.

A harder route puzzle

The guided run pauses at the crossing. Try the fast route to see the shadow catch you, or spend two extra steps to pass while it is away.

Free play lets you choose another house order, draw a different route, or return early with a smaller banked score.

What changed in Level 02

  1. Striped squares are blocked streets. Routes cannot cross them.
  2. Sixteen starting light and only two back per house make every detour matter.
  3. The purple shadow loops around the gate after the first delivery. Your route controls its timing.
  4. Bank early at the blue depot, or light all three houses for a bigger score.

Authored design study, not an Android build. The map and four-tile shadow patrol stay fixed; your path and timing change the result. This level is intentionally tight: a safe full delivery can return with only two light.
