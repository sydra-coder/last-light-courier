# Last Light Courier: trophies, competition, and Play commerce

Status: design and test plan, 6 October 2026. No production feature, Play Console configuration, payment, account, or service was created.

## Verified starting point

- `index.html` is the restored playable browser build with 200 selectable levels. Its `last-light-courier-campaign-v1` browser save holds one wallet, cleared/mastered records, hints, milestone claims, and per-level repairs. Banked points currently buy marked repairs.
- `design/home-navigation/` and `design/home-navigation/screen-mockups.html` are read-only visual mockups. Trophies summarize clears and mastery; Shop and Courier show proposed items and 18 courier concepts with five paid-look concepts each. They do not award trophies, equip owned outfits, or transact.
- The progression workbook `outputs/01a11116-d047-76b1-be23-e386d7dced2f/Last_Light_Courier_2000_Level_Progression.xlsx` marks 1–1,000 as recorded map data and 1,001–2,000 as design targets. Only 1–200 are in the playable HTML. The later archives and new mechanics are not a released 2,000-level campaign.
- `design/shop-hints-plan.md` proposes future earnable gems and item prices. Those are trial figures, not approved commerce prices. Preserve the existing points and purchased repair flags during any migration; create a separate gem ledger and never infer paid gems from points.

## Four product paths

| Path | Player experience | Required infrastructure | Limits and decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. One-person offline trophies | Named badges for unique clears, mastery, no-hint runs, and chapters, stored on the phone. Personal bests and a private progress screen. | Versioned local save, backup/export plan, trophy rule evaluator. No Play Games Services or server. | Recommended launch scope. A local score is personal motivation, not a trustworthy public rank. Reinstall or phone loss can lose progress without backup. |
| 2. Multiple local profiles, one phone | Profile picker with separate clears, wallet, hints, repairs, cosmetics, and trophy records; an on-device comparison board. | Profile IDs and separate local saves, migration of the current single save into a named/default profile. No Play Games Services or server. | Good next step if the phone is shared. A Google Play purchase belongs to the Play billing account, not automatically to one game profile; choose a household entitlement rule before commerce. Local profiles can be edited/reinstalled and must not feed public ranks. |
| 3. Cross-device competition and save | Player sign-in, Play Games achievements, leaderboards, and cloud save on Android. | Android host/bridge, Play Console game configuration, Play Games Services identity, achievement/leaderboard IDs, Saved Games and conflict handling. Play Games can supply these features without an operator-run leaderboard database. | Best later path for independent players. It does not create a common team wallet or guarantee authoritative scores. Public scoring needs stronger validation before prizes or high-stakes ranking. |
| 4. Truly shared/team progress | Members contribute to one team chapter, score, or objective across phones. | Operator-managed account/team service, membership and invitation flow, database, server-authoritative event ledger, moderation/support, privacy controls, hosting/monitoring and ongoing cost. Play Games sign-in may help identity but does not itself merge progress into a team state. | Defer until there is evidence that players want cooperation. Define ownership, removal, abuse, conflict, and shutdown policy first. |

For path 3, Play Games Services supports achievements, leaderboards, and Saved Games; its leaderboard timeframes are daily, weekly and all-time. Daily/weekly reset on Google's stated UTC-7 schedule, so show the actual period label rather than implying an India-midnight reset. Saved Games can be used offline locally and synchronize later, with explicit conflict resolution. Sources checked 6 October 2026: [Play Games overview](https://developer.android.com/games/distribute/pgs), [leaderboards](https://developer.android.com/games/pgs/leaderboards), [cloud save](https://developer.android.com/games/pgs/savedgames), [Android conflict handling](https://developer.android.com/games/pgs/android/saved-games), [Console feature setup](https://developer.android.com/games/pgs/console/enable-features).

## Trophy and competition rules to prototype

Use stable event IDs (`level-cleared`, `level-mastered`, `chapter-completed`, `hint-used`, `repair-used`, `run-ended`) and a rules version. Award once per unique level or milestone, never on page render. Suggested launch badges: First Delivery (first unique clear), Ten Roads (10 unique clears), Chapter Lantern (all ten levels in a chapter), and Clean Route (one completed level without a hint or purchased repair). Existing mastery can appear as a record, but its exact rule should be read from the active game before assigning a new badge. No badge rewards gems until the gem economy is approved.

For a local comparison board, show **unique cleared levels** and **clean mastered levels** separately. For any future public leaderboard, propose: (A) furthest contiguous cleared level from level 1, and (B) count of unique clean clears among a fixed, versioned set of eligible levels. Define clean as no hint, paid repair, tunnel, or other purchased gameplay aid in that run; mandatory free repair vouchers supplied by a level are allowed. Count each level once, taking the best eligible run. Do not rank wallet size, total points, gem purchases, or raw repeatable deliveries. Free level selection means a raw highest-level-cleared rank would misrepresent progress. If level layouts or scoring rules change, start a new leaderboard ID/season and retain old results as history. No real-money purchases should improve public rank.

Play Games has built-in leaderboard tamper protection for new Android leaderboards, but a browser local save and client-submitted score remain weak evidence. For a credible public ranking, ship only non-prize leaderboards first, attach game/rules version and eligible run summary, reject impossible level IDs and duplicate/replayed events, and review anomalies. If rankings become consequential, move run verification to an operator backend that replays deterministic move events against the authoritative level version. Never trust localStorage as proof of a paid entitlement or team contribution. [Leaderboard concepts and tamper protection](https://developer.android.com/games/pgs/leaderboards) checked 6 October 2026.

Offline behavior: local trophies and runs apply immediately and are marked pending sync. On reconnect, sync idempotently by event ID and account/profile ID. Keep the local board visible when sign-in fails; label public rank as unavailable/stale. Cloud save conflict resolution must preserve monotonic unique clears and owned cosmetics, while wallet and spend ledgers require transaction-level reconciliation; never simply add two gem balances or overwrite purchased repairs. Offer the player a clear conflict choice when automatic merge cannot prove correctness. For identity, use Play Games player identity only with sign-in, let guests play offline, display the selected profile and account before restoring, minimize stored personal data, and provide account unlink/delete and privacy disclosures before online launch.

## Commerce architecture, if later approved

The HTML mockup cannot invoke Play Billing directly. Package the game as an Android app and implement a native Billing Library bridge (or move to an Android game runtime); expose only validated catalog, purchase status, and entitlement calls to the web layer. Match the Play package and product IDs, and use store-supplied localized prices. No card entry or purchase-token verification belongs in browser JavaScript.

| Product | Play product model | Entitlement | Restore/refund behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gem packs | Consumable one-time products | Verified purchase token credits a fixed gem quantity exactly once to a durable gem ledger, then token is consumed. Earned gems and paid gems should have separate source entries even if one displayed balance is chosen. | A consumed token will not appear as an owned item on reinstall; recovery requires the operator's durable ledger/account. If that service is unavailable, do not sell consumable packs. Voids/refunds create a reversing ledger entry; spent gems require a stated debt or support policy, never silent point/repair deletion. |
| Cosmetic outfit | Non-consumable one-time product per permanent SKU (or an explicitly defined bundle) | Verified token grants immutable outfit ownership; equip is separate, per game profile. No gameplay bonus. A concept sheet is not a ready SKU. | Query current purchases on app start/resume and Restore Purchases; acknowledge rather than consume. On revoke/refund, remove the entitlement and fall back to the free Roadworn look. Preserve the account association and show a clear message. |

Lifecycle: query `ProductDetails` freshly; launch the native flow; handle success, cancel, unavailable, disconnect and `PENDING`; verify `PURCHASED` token/product/package and prior use on a secure backend before granting; record grant idempotently; consume a gem pack or acknowledge a cosmetic; retry interrupted processing on resume and from server notifications. Never grant pending or declined purchases. Google says completed purchases need acknowledgement within three days; consumption satisfies this for consumables. Use the Developer API, real-time developer notifications, and voided-purchase handling for durable paid entitlements. A client-only path exists in Google's documentation, but it cannot provide robust cross-device recovery of consumed gems and is unsuitable for the proposed paid gem ledger. Sources checked 6 October 2026: [Billing integration](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/integrate), [backend integration](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/backend), [Billing test guide](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/test).

Before implementation, decide whether a purchased outfit applies to every local profile using the buying Play account or only a linked game account. Do not attach a paid token to an arbitrary profile selected after purchase. If multiple Play accounts share a device, use the account that actually made the purchase and expose a support path for mismatches.

## Phased recommendation and acceptance gates

1. **One-phone launch:** Implement local named trophies and personal bests in a versioned save, with a read-only Trophies screen. Keep current points, repairs and hints intact. Gate: migration from `campaign-v1` yields the same wallet, cleared/mastered levels, repairs and hints; badge evaluation is idempotent across replay, reload and reset; offline and restart work; no unreleased level counts toward a trophy.
2. **If the phone is shared:** Add profile picker and an on-device comparison view. Gate: two profiles have isolated progress and spending, switching mid-run cannot transfer an award, legacy save imports exactly once, profile deletion and export are explicit, and purchase ownership behavior is decided before showing paid products.
3. **When an Android build is ready:** Add Play Games sign-in, opt-in cloud save, achievements, and non-prize leaderboards. Gate: two-device sign-in/restore, guest-to-account migration, offline edits plus reconnect, conflict and sign-out tests pass; only eligible verified 1–200 runs submit; public score does not improve from buying an aid. Publish relevant Play Games configuration through internal testing before release.
4. **If commerce is approved:** Add native Play Billing and secure purchase ledger before enabling products. Gate: license tester and internal-track success, decline, cancel, pending completion/cancellation, process kill, duplicate callback/token, repeated gem buy, cosmetic restore, refund/void, offline store failure, wrong account and reinstall tests pass. Reconcile grants/consumes/acknowledgements with backend records; no real charged test is needed for the first gate. [Google billing tests](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/test) and [internal test setup](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9845334) checked 6 October 2026. Internal-track testers can be charged unless also configured as license testers.
5. **Only after player demand:** Prototype a shared team chapter with a server ledger and explicit team rules. Gate: concurrent contribution, duplicate event, member removal, invite abuse, offline queue, conflict, outage recovery, privacy/deletion and monthly operating cost are reviewed before rollout.

### Test matrix to execute during implementation

| ID | Scenario | Pass condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T01 | Fresh save, clear same level twice | One unique-clear trophy event; no duplicated milestone or gems. |
| T02 | Use hint, repair, then finish; later finish clean | First run excluded from clean board; later eligible run recorded once. |
| T03 | Edit localStorage to inflate score/points | Public board rejects or withholds unverified result; paid entitlement unchanged. |
| T04 | Two offline devices clear different levels, then sync | Both clears retained; no duplicate award; conflicted spend requires ledger resolution. |
| T05 | Two profiles on one phone, switch/restart | Wallet, repairs, cleared levels, badges and bests stay separate. |
| B01 | Gem pack success callback twice, app killed before consume | Exactly one ledger credit; processing resumes and token is consumed. |
| B02 | Pending then canceled; pending then completed | No early grant; completed purchase grants once and is processed. |
| B03 | Outfit purchased, app reinstalled, Restore tapped | Outfit restored to eligible owner and can be equipped; no second purchase. |
| B04 | Refund/void of pack or outfit | Reversal/revocation follows written policy and is visible to player/support. |
| B05 | Play unavailable or network lost | Gameplay remains playable offline; purchase button explains unavailability and makes no local credit. |

Open product choices: (1) launch with one profile or multiple local profiles; (2) should trophies be status only or give non-competitive rewards; (3) should public competition be a later goal; (4) should paid packs ever be offered. These choices do not block the local planning and tests above.
