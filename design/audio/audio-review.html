Mix intent

Ambient motif stays quiet beneath movement and delivery sounds. Shadow proximity adds a soft dissonant breath rather than repeating the existing shadow warning. Low light adds measured pulses, never a siren. Completion opens into warm rising intervals; failure falls into a short unresolved low tone, then silence.
