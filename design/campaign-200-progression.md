# 200-level campaign progression

The original 100 maps retain their grid size and delivery objectives. Shadow introductions are permanent: one patrol in Levels 1–40; one patrol plus Echo in 41–60; two distinct, non-overlapping patrols plus Echo in 61–200. Shadows wake after the first delivery. Level 100 no longer drops back to one shadow.

| Levels | Grid | Houses required | Shadows |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 101–120 | 10 × 10 | All 4 | 2 patrols + Echo |
| 121–140 | 12 × 12 | All 5 | 2 patrols + Echo |
| 141–160 | 14 × 14 | All 6 | 2 patrols + Echo |
| 161–180 | 16 × 16 | All 7 | 2 patrols + Echo |
| 181–200 | 18 × 18 | All 8 | 2 patrols + Echo |

New boards vary winding lane lengths, orientation, house placement, patrol locations and shortcuts. Each contains a timed switch and gate, fading crossing, ice and dark street. Light margin tightens within each 20-level band. Difficulty progresses by bands; individual maps may be easier or harder than their immediate neighbors. Repair prices continue increasing. Purchases remain optional.

The map legend uses the same drawings as the game. Zoom enlarges both the scene and tile hit targets; the viewport scrolls in both directions. Existing wallet, purchases and level completion remain saved. Personal step records restart once because shadow rules changed.

Validation: all 200 guaranteed delivery routes completed without repairs using actual movement rules. Exact minimum steps with/without repair were recalculated for all 200 maps. Separate browser checks passed 200 picker entries, tutorial navigation, actual tile clicks in all three views, mobile zoom and no JavaScript errors. Android 0.1.4 builds and signature verification passed; emulator install/start verified. Physical-device sound and vibration still need player testing.
