Your first move

Choose a green tile

The green outlines show every safe adjacent move, like legal moves in chess. Tap one to move immediately. There is no Go button.

After the first house, any adjacent tile the shadow would occupy next is red and cannot be tapped.

What changes per tap

1Choose any green neighboring tile. The courier enters it and spends one light.
2The shadow advances one tile. A move that would collide with it is unavailable.
3Once used, the orange crossing counts down. When it closes, choose another road home.

Try the crossing

Press Start at choice to reach the first house quickly. The gate to its right is red and blocked because the shadow would enter it next. The other green neighbors remain available; step up and back to change the timing.
