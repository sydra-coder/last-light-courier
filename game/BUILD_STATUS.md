# Integrated game review build

Open `game/index.html` to review the current playable integration. It renders as a centered 430 × 844 phone screen on a desktop browser and uses the available viewport on a phone. It opens directly into the five-tab shell (Map, Shop, Play, Courier, Trophies). Play now starts on the approved storybook night-village scene, with saved progress and a Play button that opens the next level; this is the Play tab, not a separate splash page. Storybook is now the initial gameplay view; Isometric and Night remain selectable in Settings. The boards still need fully illustrated game art. Players tap neighboring board tiles to move; the arrow D-pad is hidden. Route hints, level picker, powers, repairs, and local save behavior come from the 2,000-level campaign preview.

Levels with a repair now show its name, point cost, and banked balance directly above the board. Opening the repair shows its effect and an explicit Spend/Keep points choice. A cancelled choice spends nothing. The Level 10 examples are `game/repair-sample.png` and `game/repair-confirmation-sample.png`.

Large maps (12 × 12 and up) now open fitted in a stable square board viewport on the phone layout. Zoom map is optional; the zoomed view follows the courier but keeps the viewport inside the actual tile bounds, avoiding the empty left strip seen at Level 1401. The board takes the width previously used by the icon rail; the ? button opens all current map symbols and explanations. The Level 1301 examples are `game/large-map-sample.png` and `game/map-symbols-sample.png`; `game/large-map-fit-after.png` and `game/large-map-zoom-after.png` show the current Level 1401 fit and zoom views.

On fitted large maps, lit houses show gold check markers and unlit houses show numbered dark markers. Repeated wall ornaments and tiny tile captions are suppressed so the route and objects are easier to scan; repair uses a small tool marker. The mobile Play screen now shows delivery and road-change messages above the board. Tapping a non-move tile reveals its full map label there. `game/large-map-clarity-sample.png` shows Level 1401 with three lit houses and an inspected closed road; `node game/large-map-clarity-smoke.cjs` checks the markers and messages.

The redundant in-level Home and Map buttons are hidden because the five-tab navigation provides those destinations. Restart is a compact control beside the board; ? contains both map symbols and How to play. The Fit/Zoom button appears only on large maps. See `game/streamlined-play-sample.png` for a small-map view.

The former Tools dropdown is now a one-row icon inventory. A short tap immediately uses a ready power; holding an icon shows its full effect and charge count without spending it. Earned tools appear on any level where they can apply, not only their assigned review map. Anchor Trap and Decoy Light act on the courier's current tile. `game/power-icons-sample.png` shows the tray and held-tooltip; `node game/power-toolbar-smoke.cjs` checks global inventory, long press, current-tile placement, and charge use. After activation, the live route check can verify simple early routes or prove some impossible light budgets. For complex maps it explicitly says the route is unverified; the required exact solver contract for future Earthquake and Storm inventory powers is in `game/POWER_AND_ROUTE_RULES.md`.

The result display now shows Your steps, Personal best, and All-player best. It does not display the calculated route minimum. Until an authenticated shared results service is connected, All-player best remains unavailable rather than showing a fabricated value. `game/player-data.js` stores a guest profile and local completed-run records; `game/PLAYER_RESULTS_PLAN.md` defines the proposed account, verification, database, and privacy work for real cross-player records. See `game/result-preview.png` for the result sample.

Level tutorials no longer appear automatically on launch or level reset. The Help control on the Play screen opens the current level explanation when requested.

The Map tab now shows a ten-stop Lantern Road for the selected chapter, with completed, current, and unplayed stops styled separately. A stop opens its level details and Play button; district and chapter controls still reach all 2,000 levels. The completion result has a visible × that closes it into the Map tab.

The level-navigation chat is still revising and auditing the campaign. Run `node game/build.cjs` after its preview changes to refresh this build; `node game/smoke.cjs` checks the level count, normal Level 1 completion, representative level loading, navigation, and browser errors.

Challenge pass: Echo Shadow no longer blocks or appears on the board. Levels 20, 40, 60 and every later 20-level milestone through 2,000 now have an additional patrol with a 6–14 tile route; Level 70 has the same treatment for direct comparison. Level 1,800's added patrol wakes after the third delivery because its dense route otherwise blocks the recorded clear. The challenge patrols are generated from `game/challenge-patrols.js` during `game/build.cjs`, so refreshed campaign data gets the same rule. `game/challenge-smoke.cjs` verifies 101 targeted maps, reference-route completion on Levels 20–100, and a Level 70 hint. `game/challenge-level70-sample.png` is a phone-sized sample. Replay testing proves those sample routes remain clearable; whether the milestones cause the desired number of retries needs player testing. Previous local best times are kept in run history but reset as current best times because the gameplay rules changed (revision 3).

This is a browser review build. Courier and outfit choices change the board appearance and save locally. One figure is now cropped from each five-look concept sheet. Shadows are assigned automatically by 100-level district from `design/shadow-variants/region-shadow-map.json`; this changes appearance only, not hazards or AI. The selected art follows positions in all three views but still needs directional frames and walking animation. Trophies read local progress. The Shop leads to the existing point-funded repairs and tools; gem checkout and Google Play billing are not connected. The 2,000 maps are candidate layouts, with late-route quality still under audit. Android packaging, physical-phone playtesting, complete art animation, online competition, and store launch remain later production work.

Courier selection uses one-screen image grids for all 18 couriers and six wardrobe looks, with no shadow picker. The grids were checked at 390 × 844 and 320 × 700 without internal scrollbars. Settings now controls background music, effects, haptics, motion, move contrast, and the three board views. The old continuous two-oscillator hum has been replaced by sparse, warm note phrases with quiet gaps. `game/soft-music-preview.html` uses the same music engine for a quick listening review. This remains procedural review music and needs listening feedback and physical-phone balance testing before final audio approval.

The restored root `index.html` and its separate 200-level save remain untouched.
