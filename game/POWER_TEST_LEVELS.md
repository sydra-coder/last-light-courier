# Power test levels

Open `tester.html` for testing. It starts with all nine powers unlocked and ten charges each. A muted icon has stock but is waiting for its map or timing condition. Tap the icon to see the condition. A gold outline means the power is ready. The player build unlocks each power only after clearing the milestone below and awards two charges.

| Power | Clear to unlock in player build | First featured test level | When the tester icon becomes ready |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Anchor Trap | 100 | 101 | After the first delivery, stand on a patrol route. |
| Decoy Light | 150 | 151 | After the first delivery, while patrols are active. |
| Reveal Pulse | 200 | 201 | On a hidden-house map, before the Beacon reveals it. |
| Lumen Flask | 300 | 1001 | After at least one light has been spent. It can also be tried on Level 301. |
| Road Repair | 400 | 402 | After the marked road has closed or broken. |
| Light Bridge | 700 | 701 | After a delivery, with enough light to build the marked crossing. |
| Map Stabilizer | 900 | 901 | While the map's marked road event can still be protected. |
| Freeze Seal | 1400 | 1401 | After the first delivery, while patrols are active. |
| Rewind | 1400 | 1402 | After making a move. |

Each power can be used once per run when its condition is met. Restart the level to try another timing. The tester and player builds save separately on this device.
