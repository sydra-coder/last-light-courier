01 / Set out

The village is waiting

The blue depot is your start and your safe return. Try the guided level, or draw a route to the nearest house yourself.

Tap one neighboring tile to begin a route.

Make the route choice

The guided level stops after the first delivery. Choose the direct road to see the shadow catch the courier, or take the upper road and continue home.

Outside the guide, every tile is yours to choose. The red preview warns of a loss, but you can still press Go.

What the symbols mean

  1. Gold houses need a delivery. Glowing houses have been lit.
  2. Purple shadow starts moving after the first delivery, one tile per courier step.
  3. Red preview warns that the shadow would catch you. You can risk it or redraw the route.
  4. Blue depot banks your unbanked points and completes the run.

Design study, not a finished game or Android build. This level uses one authored layout and a visible four-tile shadow patrol. Your route changes when and where the courier meets the shadow. Houses refill light; points are banked at the depot.
